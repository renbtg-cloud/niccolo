# Decision readiness in the MVP

Niccolò can record that a human decision is ready, not ready or should be escalated without pretending that every uncertainty has been resolved.

A readiness assessment is tied to an existing human-facing decision point and a named subject. It preserves the rationale, unresolved unknowns and bounded conditions whose future observation could reverse the decision or require review.

`READY` therefore does not mean “true beyond doubt”. It means the current investigation can support a human decision for the stated purpose while preserving material reversal conditions. The human still owns the decision and any external action.

Later evidence does not rewrite an earlier assessment. A new analysis or newer assessment can supersede its practical relevance while the older state remains auditable.

For Aurelius & Finch this supports divergent decision outcomes for Kessler, Julian, Camille, Tomas, Nadine and the Resolution Credit system, including cases where one decision is ready while another remains open.
