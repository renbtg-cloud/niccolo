# Source scope and missingness in the MVP

Niccolò represents collection scope separately from source content. A source family can be expected but not requested, requested and pending, collected, known unavailable, declared out of scope, or of unknown existence.

These states are intentionally non-equivalent. `KNOWN_UNAVAILABLE` does not mean `UNKNOWN_EXISTENCE`; `EXPECTED_NOT_REQUESTED` does not mean `COLLECTED`; and a missing family does not itself establish misconduct, sabotage or concealment.

Each source-scope assertion is attached to one frozen analysis, records who supplied it, can name the collector and date scope, and may point to one document that was actually part of that frozen Situation revision. Later corrections are represented by a new assertion that supersedes the earlier one rather than rewriting history.

The reasoning packet receives source-scope assertions so the model can avoid statements such as “no evidence exists” when the relevant material was never collected or its existence is unknown.

This capability is general. In Aurelius & Finch it lets Niccolò distinguish Elias's selected collection, an uncollected Finance source family, a retention-deleted mailbox folder and a genuinely empty search without collapsing them into the same absence.
